### 4.2 Mechanistic Interpretation of Positive and Negative `H1` Findings (Figures 2A–D & 4A–D)
1. **Why 1D velocity (`B2`) fails while conformational/pose coordinates (`Tier A–C`) excel**: Adding 1D $R_{\text{portal}}$ velocity $\Delta R(2\text{ ns})$ yields negligible skill (`BSS_int = +0.0025` on $q_+^{\text{MSM}}$; `+0.0135` on $q_+^{\text{DGA}}$), ruling out inertial 1D ballistic commitment. By contrast, `Tier C` linear Peters–Trout achieves **`BSS_int = +0.7859` (`MSM-weighted +0.6613`, leave-out MSM `+0.7731`)** on $q_+^{\text{MSM}}$ and **`+0.3497` (`\Delta\bar{\ell} = +0.0205`)** on $q_+^{\text{DGA}}$ (**Figure 2A**), ranking committors accurately *inside* every narrow `0.5 Å` stratum across `[9.5, 18.5] Å` (`within-bin Spearman rho = 0.45–0.82`, **Figure 2C**) and tightening transition-separatrix dispersion (`|\hat{q}_+ - 0.5| < 0.05`) from `std(q_ref) = 0.3071` (`B1`) down to **`0.0926`** (`Tier C`).
2. **Honest diagnosis of why `H1_Switches` (`+0.1051` on $q_+^{\text{MSM}}$, `+0.1993` on $q_+^{\text{DGA}}$) lags `Tier A` (`+0.7869`)**:
   - In the absence of $G_i$ protein, canonical intracellular GPCR microswitches remain **quiescent across ligand entry (`Figure 4C`)**: median `F200^{3.36}–W356^{6.48}` min distance stays at `3.53 -> 3.51 Å`, `F155^{2.42}–F237^{4.46}` stays at `4.08 -> 4.01 Å`, `Y294^{5.58}–Y397^{7.53}` stays at `10.77 -> 10.61 Å`, and cytoplasmic TM6 (`R214^{3.50}–D338^{6.30}` $\text{C}\alpha$) shifts by only `+0.67 Å` (`9.95 -> 10.62 Å`). Consequently, individual receptor sidechain and lipid families yield modest add-one gains over 1D $R_{\text{portal}}$ (`+0.0357` to `+0.0570` `BSS`, `95%` CIs overlapping zero; **Figure 2D**).
   - Instead, single-ensemble endocannabinoid commitment is governed by **two orthosteric/portal physical degrees of freedom**:
